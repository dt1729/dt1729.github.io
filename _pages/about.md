I am a Hardware Engineer at __Amazon Robotics__ in Westborough, MA, where I design and deploy robotic workcells for manipulation programs — spanning motor drives, motion controllers, and safety-rated EtherCAT control. My work covers low-level embedded systems (CANOpen, FSoE/SoE) through software tooling (Python HMI libraries, GenICam camera drivers).

My interests lie broadly in __robotics and autonomous systems__: motion planning, state estimation, multi-agent control, and the full stack from sensor to actuator. I hold an M.S. in Electrical Engineering from the __University of Colorado Boulder__, where I researched LLM-based root cause analysis for robotic behavior trees at the [CAIRO Lab](https://cairo-lab.github.io/), and worked on kinodynamic planning, trajectory optimization, and multi-agent formation control.

Before CU Boulder, I earned my B.E. in Instrumentation and Control Engineering from __Netaji Subhas Institute of Technology (University of Delhi)__, where I published two papers on process control. After graduation, I spent a year at IIIT Delhi as a research assistant on the [ALIVE project](https://sites.google.com/iiitd.ac.in/iiitd-alive), building and benchmarking autonomy stack components for last-mile autonomous vehicles.
